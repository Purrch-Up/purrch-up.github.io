How to play

One thumb is all it takes

Swipe up

Jump straight up to the next step.

Swipe diagonally

Hop sideways to a step on the left or right.

Hold and drag

Walk along a step to line up your jump.

The screen starts scrolling after your first jump. Fall below the bottom edge and the run is over, so keep climbing!

FAQ

Questions? We’ve got answers

Is Purrch Up! free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases include fish packs, bubbles, coats, accessories and a one-time Remove Ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can climb anywhere, no account or sign-in needed. Ads and purchases need a connection.

Which devices can play?

Android phones and tablets (Android 7.0 and up) and iPhone and iPad.

I lost my purchases on a new phone

Open Settings › Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google or Apple account. More help is on the support page.
